## Planned groups

- [ ] **Group 2** (5 experiments) — light and water
  1. **Shadows** — move a lamp around an object; the shadow grows, shrinks and turns. Scientist: distance and angle, why shadows are long in the evening.
  2. **Rainbow Prism** — shine white light through a prism to split it into colors. Scientist: rotate the prism, refraction, why red bends least.
  3. **Mirror Reflection** — bounce a light beam off mirrors to hit a target. Scientist: angle in equals angle out.
  4. **States of Water** — heat and cool water: ice, liquid, steam. Scientist: thermometer, 0 °C and 100 °C, what the particles do.
  5. **Dissolving** — stir sugar, salt, sand and oil into water. Scientist: warm water dissolves faster, saturation.

- [ ] **Group 3** (5 experiments) — forces and motion
  1. **Ramp Race** — roll a car down a ramp; change the height and the surface. Scientist: measure distance, friction.
  2. **Pendulum** — pull and release a swinging ball. Scientist: change length and weight, time 10 swings; only length matters.
  3. **Lever and Seesaw** — balance animals on a seesaw. Scientist: weight × distance on each side.
  4. **Balloon Rocket** — blow up a balloon on a string and let go. Scientist: more air goes farther, action and reaction.
  5. **Gravity Drop** — drop a feather and a ball, in air and with the air pumped out. Scientist: air resistance, not weight, makes the difference.

- [ ] **Group 4** (5 experiments) — electricity, sound, nature and space
  1. **Static Electricity** — rub a balloon on hair, then pick up paper bits. Scientist: charges, more rubbing means more pull.
  2. **Sound Waves** — pluck strings of different lengths. Scientist: see the wave, pitch (frequency) and loudness (amplitude).
  3. **Density Tower** — pour honey, water and oil into a glass; they stack in layers. Scientist: drop objects in and see where each one stops.
  4. **Plant Growth** — give a seed water and light and watch it grow. Scientist: change one thing at a time, a fair test.
  5. **Day and Night** — spin the Earth next to the Sun. Scientist: time zones, why the Sun seems to rise in the east.
